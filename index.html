03 / Processing

Washed, Natural or Honey.

Each method treats the cherry differently, so you can compare lots by process as well as variety.

Washed

Skin and mucilage are removed before drying. Clean and structured, letting the variety and origin come through.

Natural

Dried whole, inside the cherry. Can emphasise sweetness, fruit character and body.

Honey

Skin removed, with mucilage left on during drying. Can balance traits of washed and natural coffees.
