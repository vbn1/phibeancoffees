Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you send an inquiry through the form or the chat assistant, we receive the details you enter: your name, company or farm name, professional email address, phone number (optional), opportunity type, preferred variety and process, quantity, cup profile and any notes. The chat assistant uses the same form, and sends nothing until you confirm the summary.

With each inquiry we also record how you reached the site: the referring website, the landing page path and any campaign (UTM) parameters in the link.

How we use it

We use inquiry details only to reply to you and to discuss samples, lots, contracts, estate visits or grower registration. Source information helps us understand which channels bring relevant enquiries. We do not sell your information.

Services we rely on

  • Web3Forms processes inquiry submissions and delivers them to us by email.
  • Umami provides site analytics (page views and a few events such as an inquiry being submitted). It is configured to count visits without tracking individuals across sites.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces, which means your browser contacts Google's servers when the page loads.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep standard server logs.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you sent us. Send an inquiry through the inquiry form and select the closest opportunity type, and mention your request in the details.
